The 🌌 Horizon Team and the 🛡️ Mods run these two dashboards together. Here is how they work. Back to the dashboards.

Signing in

  • Run /staff password on Horizon's Discord: Kira DMs you a link that works once, for 30 minutes. Choose a password of 12 characters or more.
  • Sign in with your Discord username. "Sign out everywhere" ends every session you have. If you lose the Team or Mod role, your sessions end at once.

Applications and the vote

  • Who votes. 🌌 Team applications: the Team. 🛡️ Mod applications: the Mods. 🤝 Support applications: the Mods and the Team. Nobody votes on their own application, and you can recuse yourself.
  • Your vote. For, Against or Abstain. Against needs a short comment. You can change your vote until the vote closes.
  • Blind until you vote. You see the others' votes and comments only once you have voted, or when the vote closes, so nobody just follows the crowd.
  • Enough votes. An active voter is someone who signed in or acted here in the last 30 days. A vote needs at least 2 votes, and at least 40% of the active voters. With fewer than 2 active voters, Ludovic decides.
  • Passing. At least two thirds of the For and Against votes are For (abstentions don't count either way), with enough votes.
  • How long. 72 hours from the first vote, or less once every active voter has voted. Without enough votes it gets 48 more hours once; after that it goes to Ludovic.
  • Verified contributors (GitHub linked and proved on Discord) are listed first, with their merged pull requests.

Ludovic's veto

  • Ludovic can veto any application at any time, while it is voted on or after it passed. A veto declines it; no reason is needed.
  • When a vote passes, Kira DMs him and a 12-hour window opens. If he does nothing, the role is given when it ends and the person gets a DM. He can also approve at once.
  • After a role is given he can still take it back from the dashboard: a normal removal, written in the log.
  • A 🌌 Team acceptance gives only the Discord role. Access to the GitHub organisation is Ludovic's to give, by hand.

The log and privacy

  • Every vote, change, recusal, veto and decision is written in a log that can't be edited. Voters and Ludovic see it under "More" on each application.
  • The applicant only gets a polite yes or no in their DMs: never the votes, the comments or who voted. After a no, they can apply again in 14 days.
  • Answers, comments and GitHub details are deleted 90 days after the decision; only the role, the outcome and the date stay (privacy).
  • The statistics are counts only: no messages, nothing from anyone's private DMs.
